## 1. CORE VISUAL IDENTITY & THEME

Monsoon Mayhem is set in the chaotic, rain-drenched streets of Kolkata during peak monsoon. The art style blends **semi-realistic 3D stylized models** with **vibrant arcade readability**. High-contrast wet reflections, deep rain waterlogging, and culturally rich Kolkata urban props create an immersive, hilarious, and intense survival experience.

---

## 2. MAIN CHARACTER — THE RIDER

* **Identity:** Young Bengali male commuter racing against time to reach home.
* **Attire:**
  * **Raincoat:** Bright canary yellow hooded waterproof raincoat with front zipper and flap pockets.
  * **Helmet:** Glossy black open-face retro motorcycle helmet with black chin strap.
  * **Backpack:** Compact black waterproof dual-strap office/college bag worn on back.
  * **Bottom:** Denim blue rolled-up trousers/jeans.
  * **Footwear:** Tan/brown water-resistant sneakers/shoes.
* **Character Expressions (HUD & Death Replay):**
  * **Happy / Confident:** Broad smile, enjoying the ride (`Near Miss` / `Clean Dodge`).
  * **Shocked:** Wide eyes, open mouth (`Bus Wave Incoming!` / `Pothole Ahead!`).
  * **Angry:** Clenched jaw, furrowed brow (`Crowd Shove` / `Traffic Block`).
  * **Scared / Panicked:** Screaming face, sweating, terror (`Deep Manhole` / `Engine Stall`).

## 3. VEHICLE SPECIFICATIONS

### Starting Vehicle: Vintage Bajaj Chetak (Blue Classic Scooter)
* **Body:** Sky-blue / vintage aqua curved steel monocoque chassis.
* **Front:** Classic circular halogen headlight with chrome bezel, curved apron/leg-shield, white front indicator lenses.
* **Rear:** Bulbous teardrop side cowls, round red taillight, mounted rear spare wheel, kick-starter, chrome exhaust pipe with visible water-exhaust sputtering.
* **License Plate:** Yellow West Bengal registration plate (`WB 02 ...`).

### Future Unlockable Vehicles:
* **Hero Splendor:** Classic black commuter motorcycle with silver alloy rims.
* **Bajaj RE Auto-Rickshaw:** Classic green and yellow 3-wheeler with canvas canopy roof.
* **Kolkata City Bus:** Private commuter bus in vibrant blue and yellow livery with grill-protected rear window.
* **Boat Scooter:** Floating amphibious modification for deep water zones.

## 4. ENVIRONMENT & LEVEL THEMES (KOLKATA MONSOON)

1. **Old Kolkata Street (Narrow Lane):**
   * Heritage colonial architecture, green wooden shuttered windows, wrought iron balconies.
   * Tangled overhead electrical wires, tea stalls (Cha-er Dokan), wet brick walls.
2. **Market Area (Bazar Chaos):**
   * Crowded sidewalk shops, fruit & vegetable stalls, wet canvas awnings, neon signs.
   * Red postal pillar boxes, hand-pulled rickshaws, street food carts ("Bhaat Gorom").
3. **Main Highway / Central Avenue:**
   * Wide 4-lane asphalt road submerged in varying water depths.
   * Heavy vehicular traffic (Buses, Lorries, Taxis, Autos), streetlights with glowing yellow halos.
4. **Night Ride (Neon Lights):**
   * Dark stormy sky illuminated by periodic lightning flashes.
   * Vibrant neon signage reflecting on flooded asphalt, glowing vehicle headlights and red brake lights.

## 5. ROAD & WATER VISUALS

* **Road Surface:** Dark charcoal wet asphalt, cracked road patches, worn white lane markings, zebra crossings.
* **Flooded Water Layer:**
  * **Shallow (0-15cm):** Subtle road sheen, tire ripples, light spray.
  * **Moderate (15-30cm):** Continuous water surface, floating plastic bottles/garbage, reduced scooter traction.
  * **Deep (30-50cm+):** Heavy water wake, submerged potholes, rising engine stress.
* **Obstacles & Hazards:**
  * **Potholes:** Submerged circular road depressions with dark center water and perimeter ripples.
  * **Open Manholes:** Circular iron rings with rotating water vortex and warning bubbles.
  * **Traffic Barricades:** Yellow, black, and red diagonal striped police barricades.
  * **Road Cones:** Bright orange cones with dual white reflective collars.
  * **Fallen Trees:** Snapped monsoon tree branches resting across lane boundaries.

---

## 6. WEATHER & ATMOSPHERIC EFFECTS

* **Heavy Rain:** Dense diagonal semi-transparent blue-white rain streaks rushing across the screen.
* **Water Splash:** Radial droplets and splash sprites when tires hit potholes or water waves.
* **Horizon Fog / Haze:** Volumetric blue-grey mist obscuring the vanishing point.
* **Lightning:** Instantaneous screen-wide white/violet flash illuminating road buildings in silhouette.

---

## 7. UI THEME & DESIGN LANGUAGE

* **Color Palette:**
  * **Primary Accent:** Vivid Gold/Yellow (`#FACC15`)
  * **Monsoon Cyan:** Deep Sky Blue (`#0284C7` / `#38BDF8`)
  * **Hazard Danger:** Signal Red (`#EF4444`)
  * **Safe / Speed:** Emerald Green (`#22C55E`)
  * **Background Surface:** Deep Slate / Obsidian (`#0F172A` / `#020617`)
* **Typography:**
  * **Headings & Buttons:** `Fredoka` (Bold, rounded, playful arcade aesthetic).
  * **Telemetry & Gauges:** `JetBrains Mono` (Technical, readable monospace numbers).
* **Speedometer:** Curved arc dial with green-yellow-red warning thresholds.

---

## 8. VISUAL CONSISTENCY MANDATES

1. **Rider Continuity:** The rider must always wear the yellow raincoat and black helmet across all scenes.
2. **Vehicle Fidelity:** The vintage blue Chetak must retain its round headlight, bulbous cowls, and rear spare tire across 3D and 2D views.
3. **No Unrelated Cartoon Assets:** No generic emojis or flat flat-colored clipart; maintain the painted 3D / pseudo-3D aesthetic.
4. **Lighting Consistency:** All specular reflections on the road and vehicles must match the overcast/stormy sky lighting.
